FORGED

A workout builder that forges a plan around you.

Answer six quick questions: how many days you can train, how long your sessions are, your experience, what gym access you have, how hard you can push, and your goal. FORGED then generates a balanced, fatigue-aware weekly plan with exercises, sets, reps and rest times.

  • tailored to your experience, time & equipment
  • picks the right split: full body, upper/lower, PPL…
  • fatigue tracking, daily workout & history log
  • no backend, no frameworks: pure vanilla JS
